## Building

### [Yotakes](https://yotakes.vercel.app) &nbsp;·&nbsp; <sub>live in production</sub>

The Letterboxd of restaurants — a trust network for people who actually eat out, built on
short reviews with photos instead of anonymous star averages. Seeded with 535 São Paulo
restaurants, row-level security on every table, dual-mode auth.

<sub>`Next.js 16` · `TypeScript` · `Supabase` · `PostgreSQL` · `Tailwind` · `PostHog`</sub>

### Arremataí &nbsp;·&nbsp; <sub>private</sub>

Arbitrage between Brazilian Federal Revenue auctions and Mercado Livre. Scrapes auction
lots, analyzes them with an LLM, computes the maximum profitable bid, then publishes the
won items back to the marketplace. Multi-tenant by JWT, Fernet-encrypted OAuth tokens,
147 tests against a real Postgres.

<sub>`Python` · `FastAPI` · `SQLAlchemy` · `pgvector` · `Next.js` · `Docker`</sub>
